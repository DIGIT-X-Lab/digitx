About this study

This concept animation describes the lab’s research direction. Values are illustrative, not measurements from the rendered anatomy. The table and graph reuse the same fictional records. The highlighted routes and KEPLER dialogue are an authored research concept, not a live system run. Displayed comparisons are calculated from the fictional records; no causal conclusion or validated clinical capability is implied. The story plays once; KEPLER’s orb and border continue until paused. Each stage can also be explored without motion.
