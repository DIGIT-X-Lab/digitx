# DIGITX editorial foundation | approved strategic direction

**Identity:** DIGITX is an academic research lab in the Department of Radiology at LMU University Hospital. Principal Investigator: Prof. Lalith Kumar Shiyam Sundar.

**Purpose:** Understand how the human body works, in health and disease.

**Scientific bet:** More of the data medicine collects can be made computable. Together with increasingly capable AI systems, this may let researchers investigate questions that are currently difficult to study. DIGITX develops both methods to make data useful for computation and research systems to work with it. Physiological modelling is substantive science in its own right, not just preprocessing.

**What this is not:** A PET-only group; a service converting clinical records into spreadsheets; a foundation-model developer; a commercial clinical AI vendor; a GPU-backed public service. Total-body PET is part of the PI's scientific history and an ongoing research area, not the lab's umbrella identity.

**Voice:** Interested before impressive. Plain and specific. Able to say what is not known. Technical vocabulary where it earns its place. No promotional superlatives, contrived humility, dramatic claims about autonomous discovery, forced frameworks, or claims that all medical data is unstructured. Use British spelling and no em dashes.

**Core question, to use once prominently:** “What can we understand about the human body when more of its data becomes computable?”

**Editorial rule:** Let a scientific result, figure, tool or named contribution prove capability. Copy should never claim beyond what linked evidence demonstrates.

**Permanent line for collaboration:** “We'll listen first.”

**Content distinction:** Research questions are enduring; funded projects change; tools are concrete contributions; publications are evidence; people have individual voices. Keep these distinct without isolating them into artificial pillars.
