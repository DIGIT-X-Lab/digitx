# Publications | public copy and presentation

**H1:** Publications

**Intro:** Questions we’ve followed, and what we’ve learned along the way.

**Controls:** One search field (title, topic, author or journal) and direct year buttons (All, then years newest first). Clear search inside the field; All resets the year. Show a compact centred numbered pager only when more than one page exists. Beyond three years, keep the newest two visible and put older years in a compact selector. No explanatory footer. Fit complete records into the available viewport, accounting for the heading, filters, repeated year labels and pagination. Recalculate page boundaries when the reading width or viewport height changes, keeping the current paper on the resulting page. On small screens, a record taller than the available space stays complete and scrolls normally. Group by year, newest first.

**Record fields:** Title, author list, year, venue, DOI, publication type/status, persistent URL, associated tools, research area, contribution attribution. Import these from real records only. **CONTENT REQUIRED: verified bibliography.**

**Archive presentation, approved 29 October 2026:** A warm ivory surface with compact, full-width strips containing year, full paper title, journal and a plus. All papers begin closed; clicking a strip opens its summary, group authors, affiliation notes and paper link underneath. Only one paper opens at a time, and its plus becomes a minus. Show positive sourced citation counts inside the expanded detail; omit unavailable counts. Use quiet dividers and a short vertical reveal, with immediate changes for reduced motion. Page capacity is measured from collapsed strips only; opening a paper must not alter page boundaries. No selected-paper or figure showcase.

**Summary provenance:** Sourced summaries and verified group authors are maintained in `src/data/publication-notes.ts`, separately from the automated bibliography. Each entry links its primary evidence. Show only the published version when a preprint is superseded. Imported missing citation counts are displayed as unavailable, never invented.

**Attribution rule:** Clearly distinguish work conducted at the current LMU lab, earlier PI contributions, and wider collaborations; never make all prior work appear to originate from DIGITX.

**Import scope:** Original journal papers from 2025 onward. Europe PMC supplies metadata and citation counts; reviewed summaries remain editorial. Only the PI's papers are in scope for now. Do not add other team members until the user supplies their Google Scholar profiles. Initials or surnames alone are not evidence of authorship. Keep these operational notes off the public page.
