# Editorial launch gate

**Not cleared by this package:** Final team roster, researcher answers and portraits; verified complete bibliography; legal imprint/privacy terms; current public contact details; full tool registry and exact licensing; tool interface definitions; active funded project periods/partners; rights-cleared figures and figure captions; independent confirmation of project status; validation scope for any demonstrated agent workflow.

**Before deployment:**

1. Check every scientific sentence against an approved source or explicitly label it a research aim.
2. Check source code, status, licence, maintainer, support expectation and hardware needs individually for each tool.
3. Check each funded project for active status, correct funder, public partner permission and period.
4. Confirm research visuals: source, rights, patient privacy, citation, what is directly observed versus computed or modelled.
5. Have each researcher approve their own profile; no invented first-person quotes.
6. Verify full bibliography and distinguish earlier PI work from current DIGITX institutional outputs.
7. Verify LMU identity rules and German site legal/privacy requirements with the institution.
8. Review pages for claims about AI ability; do not equate model outputs with biological knowledge.
9. Confirm all copy uses British spelling, no em dashes, no hype and no lab-wide claims based on one PET example.
10. Remove all internal `VERIFY`, `CONTENT REQUIRED`, and `NOT PUBLIC` tags from production surfaces and hide incomplete modules.

**Default if an asset or fact is missing:** Keep the design slot in development. On the public site, omit the module or field rather than fill with fictional research.
